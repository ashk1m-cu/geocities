    06   Coffee Diary

    Oct 3 - the place on the corner

    Found a coffeehouse two blocks from my stairs. Mismatched chairs, a chalkboard menu, and a cat who owns the radiator. I got a large black and stayed three hours. Nobody minded.

    Nov 11 - rain

    It rained sideways all afternoon so I held the door for six people with soggy umbrellas and then took the window seat. Best part of the week. The glass was fogged and the taxis were just yellow smears.

    Dec 1 - espresso experiments

    Tried three shots to finish this page. Typed so fast the keys were smoking. Do not recommend. Do recommend the biscotti.
